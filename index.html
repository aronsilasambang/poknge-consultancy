Welcome to Poknge Consultancy

Professional support for every stage of your business and career

Poknge Consultancy is a Papua New Guinean business support service that helps individuals and businesses present themselves professionally.

We prepare clear, well-designed career documents, business documents and marketing materials, and we guide clients through the steps of registering and setting up a business. We are registered under the Business Names Act with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA), based in Jiwaka Province, and serve clients across Papua New Guinea through WhatsApp, email and in person.
