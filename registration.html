Registration Support

Start your business on the right foot

Registering correctly is an important step towards operating legally, opening a business bank account, applying for contracts and building trust with customers.

The process can feel complicated if you are doing it for the first time. We guide you through each step, help you prepare complete and accurate information, and explain what to expect — so you can avoid common mistakes and delays caused by missing information.

IPA Business Name Registration & Renewal

Service fee from K150

Registering your business name is often the first formal step in starting a business in Papua New Guinea. We guide you through the IPA business name process and help make sure your application information is complete and accurate.

We can help you with

  • Choosing a suitable business name and checking whether it is available
  • Preparing the owner and business details required for the application
  • Guidance on using the IPA online registration system
  • Helping you prepare and submit renewal applications on time
  • Organising copies of your certificate and business records
Business name approval is decided by the Investment Promotion Authority. Official IPA fees are paid separately by the client.

IRC TIN Registration

Service fee from K150

A Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is needed for many business and tax activities in Papua New Guinea. We help individuals, sole traders and small businesses complete the correct TIN application for the Internal Revenue Commission.

We can help you with

  • Identifying the right TIN application form for your situation
  • Completing the application form accurately
  • Preparing a checklist of the supporting documents you need
  • Guidance on how and where to submit your application
  • Explaining the next steps after you apply
TINs are issued by the Internal Revenue Commission. We provide application assistance only and do not act as a registered tax agent.

GST Registration Guidance

Quote on request

Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration may be required depending on your type of business and turnover, as set by the IRC. We explain the process in simple terms and help you prepare your registration information.

We can help you with

  • Explaining in plain language how GST registration works
  • Helping you check the IRC requirements that may apply to your business
  • Preparing the information and documents needed for registration
  • Guidance on completing and submitting the GST registration form
This is general guidance, not tax advice. For tax advice, please consult a registered tax agent or accountant. GST registration is approved by the IRC.

Be Prepared

Documents you may need

Having these ready helps the process move faster. Exact requirements are set by each authority or bank and may change — we will confirm the current list with you before we begin.

  • Valid photo identification (for example, a National ID card, passport or driver’s licence)
  • Your contact details and residential or business address
  • A short description of your business activities
  • Your business name certificate (for TIN, GST and bank applications, if already registered)
  • Any letters or forms you have already received from the authority or bank
