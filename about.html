About Poknge Consultancy

A 100% PNG citizen-owned business support service, registered under the Business Names Act and based in Port Moresby and Jiwaka Province.

Who We Are

Helping Papua New Guineans present themselves professionally

Poknge Consultancy is a Papua New Guinean business support service that helps individuals and businesses present themselves professionally.

We prepare clear, well-designed career documents, business documents and marketing materials, and we guide clients through the steps of registering and setting up a business. We also help travellers book flights and check in online.

The business was registered with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA) under the Business Names Act on 5 November 2026. It is 100% PNG citizen-owned and operated by its owner, Aron Silas. We are based in Port Moresby (National Capital District) and Jiwaka Province, and serve clients across Papua New Guinea by WhatsApp and email.
