How It Works

How to book with our help

Simple steps, and nothing is booked until you approve it.

  1. 1

    Send your travel details

    Message us on WhatsApp with your route, travel dates and the names of everyone travelling.

  2. 2

    We check and confirm

    We check the available flights and fares, then confirm the flight, the airfare and our service fee with you.

  3. 3

    We make the booking

    Only after you approve, we complete the booking and explain how to pay the airfare and our service fee.

  4. 4

    Receive your travel documents

    We send your e-ticket to your phone. Before you fly, we can check you in online and send your boarding pass.

What we need from you

  • Full names of all travellers, exactly as shown on their ID or passport
  • Where you are flying from and to, and your preferred dates and times
  • A phone number and email address for the booking
  • For check-in: your booking reference (the code on your e-ticket) and surname
  • For international flights: passport details for each traveller

Questions

Travel questions

Are you an Air Niugini or PNG Air office or agent?

No. Poknge Consultancy is an independent business. We help customers book and check in using the airlines’ own official booking and check-in services. Matters such as refunds, schedule changes and baggage are decided by the airline.

Is your service fee included in the airfare?

No. The airfare is set by the airline. Our service fee is separate and covers our help only. We confirm both amounts with you before we make any booking.

Can you check me in if I booked my ticket myself?

Yes. Send us your booking reference and surname on WhatsApp, and we will check you in online if online check-in is available for your flight.

How will I receive my e-ticket and boarding pass?

We send them to your phone by WhatsApp or email. Please check that your name, flight number, date and time are correct.

Important information about travel services

Poknge Consultancy is an independent private business. We are not an appointed agent, office or partner of Air Niugini and PNG Air, and we do not act on behalf of any airline. We help customers use the airlines’ own booking and check-in services. Airfares, seat availability, schedules, baggage rules, changes, refunds and boarding are decided by the airline. Airfares and airline charges are separate from our service fee. Please check your name, dates and flight details carefully before you approve a booking.

Planning a trip?

Send us your travel details on WhatsApp. We will check the flights and confirm everything with you before booking.
