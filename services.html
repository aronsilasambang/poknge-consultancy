Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I request a service?

Send us a message on WhatsApp, email us, or complete the enquiry form on our Contact page. Tell us what you need and we will reply with the next steps and a quote.

How much do your services cost?

Please see our price list. Prices are indicative and may vary depending on document length, complexity, urgency and your requirements. We confirm the price with you before any work begins.

Do your fees include government or bank fees?

No. Our fees cover our assistance and document preparation only. Official fees charged by the IPA, IRC, banks or other organisations are separate and are paid by the client.

Can you guarantee that my registration or job application will be approved?

No. Registrations and approvals are decided only by the relevant authority, and hiring decisions are made by employers. Our job is to make sure your documents are complete, accurate and professionally presented.

Can I use your services if I am not in Jiwaka?

Yes. We are based in Jiwaka Province and serve clients across Papua New Guinea through WhatsApp, email and in person.

How long does it take?

It depends on the service and how quickly you can provide the information we need. We give you an estimated timeframe with your quote. Government processing times are set by the relevant authority.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We use your personal and business information only to prepare your documents and to communicate with you about your work. We do not share it with anyone else without your permission.

What format will I receive my documents in?

Final copies are provided as PDF, Word or image files, depending on the service. Designs are suitable for printing and for sharing on Facebook and WhatsApp.

Not sure which service you need?

Send us a short message describing your situation and we will recommend the right service for you.
